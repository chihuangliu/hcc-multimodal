# 1. Task

Replicate the 0525 raw experiments (`6a1a1bdf` λ=0.1, `982a6fa2` λ=0) with **tumour bounding-box cropped MRI** (`raw_bbox` mode) instead of the full raw arterial volume. The bounding box is computed from `hcc_seg_reg*.nii.gz` (binary tumour segmentation masks, co-registered to the arterial phase), resampled to 1×1×3 mm, padded by 10 voxels on each face, and clipped to the volume boundary. All other hyperparameters are identical to the corresponding 0525 raw runs.

Downstream CV is evaluated under two inference conditions:

| Condition | Train slices | Inference slices |
|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| 1 | 10 | 10 |
| 2 | 10 | all (within bbox) |

**Segmentation file coverage**: all 54 downstream patients have a `hcc_seg_reg*.nii.gz` file perfectly co-registered (same shape) with `MRI_liver_arterial.nii.gz`. File variants: `_pt` (34 patients), `_mv` (19), `_mv_flag` (1). Priority at load time: `_pt` → `_mv` → `_mv_flag`.

**Bounding box sizes** (resampled 1×1×3 mm space, +10 voxel pad): median tumour bbox ~34×36×14 voxels before padding; with padding roughly 54×56×34 voxels — approximately 10–20× smaller than the full resampled volume (~380×380×80).

# 2. Key findings

Bbox cropping consistently and substantially improves over full-volume raw runs. The λ=0.1 bbox model (`050d401d`) is the clear winner: embeddings LR at condition 1 reaches **0.988 ± 0.017** (+0.236 over the raw full-volume equivalent), and unlike the full-volume setting the concat and ensemble tasks also benefit strongly.

At condition 2 (pooling all bbox slices at inference), λ=0.1 bbox still outperforms the full-volume condition 2 by a large margin, and the degradation from condition 1 → condition 2 is much smaller than in the full-volume case — the encoder trained on focused tumour patches is more robust to deeper pooling within the same region.

The λ=0 bbox model (`e12b0592`) improves over the full-volume λ=0 baseline but lags behind the λ=0.1 bbox model, consistent with the pattern seen in the 0525 raw runs.

Best embeddings AUC across all settings (in-CV):

| | Raw full (0525) | BBox (0525) |
|---|---|---|
| Radiomics baseline | RF: 0.569 ± 0.133 | RF: 0.569 ± 0.133 |
| λ=0.1, cond 1 (10/10) | RF: 0.798 ± 0.081 | LR: **0.988 ± 0.017** |
| λ=0.1, cond 2 (10/all) | RF: 0.672 ± 0.090 | RF: **0.965 ± 0.026** |
| λ=0,   cond 1 (10/10) | LR: 0.706 ± 0.099 | LR: **0.880 ± 0.067** |
| λ=0,   cond 2 (10/all) | — | LR: 0.657 ± 0.100 |

# 3. Methodology

## 3.1 Contrastive pre-training

**Data**: `Resections_with_rna/MRI_liver_arterial.nii.gz` cropped to tumour bounding box

**Raw bbox preprocessing** (applied at dataset construction + load time, per volume):
- Resample seg mask (`hcc_seg_reg*.nii.gz`) to 1×1×3 mm via linear interpolation; threshold at >0.5 → binary
- Compute tight 3D bounding box of non-zero voxels; pad by 10 voxels per face; clip to volume boundary
- Resample MRI to 1×1×3 mm; crop to pre-computed bbox
- Per-slice: clip at 99th percentile, rescale to [0, 1]

Bbox coordinates are pre-computed once at dataset construction (54 seg resamples); the MRI is resampled and cropped at each `__getitem__` call (or loaded from the `mri_resampled` cache if available).

All encoder architecture and training settings match the 0525 raw runs:

| Parameter | `050d401d` (bbox λ=0.1) | `e12b0592` (bbox λ=0) | `6a1a1bdf` (raw λ=0.1) | `982a6fa2` (raw λ=0) |
|-----------|-------------------------|----------------------|------------------------|----------------------|
| Backbone | ViT-B/32 (unfrozen) | ViT-B/32 (unfrozen) | ViT-B/32 (unfrozen) | ViT-B/32 (unfrozen) |
| Embed dim | 128 | 128 | 128 | 128 |
| Gene hidden dim | 256 | 256 | 256 | 256 |
| Temperature τ | 0.07 | 0.07 | 0.07 | 0.07 |
| λ (reg weight) | 0.1 | 0.0 | 0.1 | 0.0 |
| MRI type | raw_bbox | raw_bbox | raw | raw |
| bbox_pad | 10 voxels | 10 voxels | — | — |
| Slices per patient | 10 (sagittal axis=0) | 10 (sagittal axis=0) | 10 (sagittal axis=0) | 10 (sagittal axis=0) |
| Image size | 224 × 224 | 224 × 224 | 224 × 224 | 224 × 224 |
| Epochs | 50 | 50 | 50 | 50 |
| Batch size | 32 | 32 | 32 | 32 |
| Optimiser | AdamW (lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4) | AdamW (lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4) | AdamW (lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4) | AdamW (lr=1e-4, wd=1e-4) |
| LR schedule | Cosine (T_max=50) | Cosine (T_max=50) | Cosine (T_max=50) | Cosine (T_max=50) |
| Seed | 42 | 42 | 42 | 42 |
| Train loss (epoch 50) | -0.389 | 0.626 | 1.330 | 1.415 |
| Val loss (best) | 0.396 (ep 49) | 1.637 | 1.327 | 3.941 (early) |

The negative train loss for `050d401d` reflects the regularisation term dominating as the contrastive term saturates on small, focus-cropped patches. The val loss remains positive (0.396), indicating the model generalises. The large train/val gap for `e12b0592` mirrors the λ=0 pattern seen in the raw runs, consistent with NT-Xent overfitting without regularisation.

## 3.2 Embedding extraction

Identical to 0525: encoders frozen, sagittal slices per patient mean-pooled → 128-dim image embedding, concatenated with 128-dim gene embedding → 256-dim joint embedding. 54 patients with paired MRI, RNA-seq, and clinical outcome.

Condition 1: 10 slices sampled uniformly within the bbox (same as training).
Condition 2: all slices within the bbox (variable per patient; mean bbox depth ~34 voxels in resampled space with padding).

Embeddings cached to `training/contrastive/{model_id}/cached_embeddings/emb_{n10,nall}.csv`.

---

## 3.3 Downstream CV pipeline

Identical to 0525: 3-fold stratified CV (`StratifiedKFold`, `random_state=42`), `SelectKBest(f_classif, k=100)` fitted on the training fold only, `StandardScaler` inside pipeline.
